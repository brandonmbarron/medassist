# PPO-GOLD 2026 Summary of Benefits (FICTIONAL, for demo only)

## Plan basics
Monthly premium: $0. Annual in-network out-of-pocket maximum: $4,900. Combined in- and out-of-network maximum: $7,500. No referral needed to see specialists.

## Primary care and specialist visits
Primary care visit: $0 copay in network, $40 out of network. Specialist visit: $35 copay in network, $70 out of network.

## Diagnostic imaging
X-rays: $15 copay. MRI, CT and PET scans: $150 copay per scan in network. Prior authorization is required for MRI, CT and PET scans.

## Lab services
Lab work such as blood panels: $0 copay in network.

## Emergency and urgent care
Emergency room: $110 copay, waived if admitted within 24 hours. Urgent care: $40 copay. Emergency care is covered worldwide. In a medical emergency, call 911.

## Physical therapy
Outpatient physical therapy: $30 copay per visit. Prior authorization is required after the 12th visit in a calendar year.

## Preventive care
Annual wellness visit, screening colonoscopy and flu shots: $0 copay in network.

## Dental, vision and hearing
Two preventive dental cleanings per year: $0. Routine eye exam: $0, plus a $200 eyewear allowance. Hearing aids: up to $1,000 allowance per ear every 3 years.

## Prior authorization
Your provider submits prior authorization requests. Standard decisions are made within 7 calendar days; expedited requests within 72 hours.

## Appeals and grievances
You may file an appeal within 65 days of a denial notice. Grievances about service quality can be filed by phone or in writing at any time.
