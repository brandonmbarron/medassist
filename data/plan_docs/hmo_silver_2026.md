# HMO-SILVER 2026 Summary of Benefits (FICTIONAL, for demo only)

## Plan basics
Monthly premium: $0. Annual in-network out-of-pocket maximum: $6,700. Out-of-network care is not covered except for emergencies. A referral from your primary care provider is required to see a specialist.

## Primary care and specialist visits
Primary care visit: $5 copay. Specialist visit: $45 copay with referral.

## Diagnostic imaging
X-rays: $25 copay. MRI, CT and PET scans: $250 copay per scan. Prior authorization is required for MRI, CT and PET scans.

## Lab services
Lab work: $10 copay.

## Emergency and urgent care
Emergency room: $125 copay, waived if admitted. Urgent care: $50 copay. In a medical emergency, call 911.

## Physical therapy
Outpatient physical therapy: $40 copay per visit. Prior authorization required before the first visit.

## Preventive care
Annual wellness visit and screening colonoscopy: $0 copay.

## Dental, vision and hearing
One preventive dental cleaning per year: $0. Routine eye exam: $0, no eyewear allowance. Hearing aids are not covered.

## Prior authorization
Your provider submits prior authorization requests. Standard decisions are made within 7 calendar days; expedited requests within 72 hours.

## Appeals and grievances
You may file an appeal within 65 days of a denial notice. Grievances can be filed by phone or in writing.
